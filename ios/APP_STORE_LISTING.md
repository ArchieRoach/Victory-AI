## Description

```
Get better at boxing, every round. Prop up your phone, press start, and your AI coach calls your combos, counts your punches and scores what it sees.

TRAIN WITH A PARTNER WHO KNOWS YOU
• Pick your partner's style and name. They coach you through every round, out loud.
• Combo calls in plain words ("Jab, cross, hook"), or numbers if your coach taught you those. They start at your level and get harder as you land them.
• Live Coach counts your punches, combos, guard drops and head movement as you go. It runs on your phone, and nothing is uploaded.
• Bag work, pads, circuits and HIIT: save your own round timer, then film yourself with the timer, bells and coach on the same screen.

SEE HOW GOOD YOU REALLY ARE
• Turn on video and the AI scores your round on 16 parts of your boxing, from jab to footwork. Every score shows the moment it saw, and anything it didn't see isn't scored.
• Get a scouting report, homework drills and personal bests to beat next time.
• Your Hours ladder counts proven training time, badge by badge.

TRAIN TOGETHER
• Join a squad, take on squad missions and cheer each other on.
• Keep a weekly streak: two training days a week keeps it alive.
• Climb the season ranks, face the weekly Boss Round and chase the Golden Gloves.
• Go live and let the community watch you train.

BUILT FOR YOUR IPHONE
• Siri: "Start a round in Victory AI".
• Round timer on your Lock Screen and Dynamic Island.
• AirPods head-motion tracking for slips and rolls.

VICTORY AI PRO
Free accounts get a monthly allowance of AI feedback. Pro unlocks unlimited AI feedback and season tables. Pro is an auto-renewing subscription charged to your Apple ID. It renews unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the period. Manage or cancel it in your App Store account settings.

Terms: https://app.victoryai.co.uk/terms
Privacy: https://app.victoryai.co.uk/privacy
```
